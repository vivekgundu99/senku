# Senku

Senku is an offline desktop companion for students built to solve a simple problem:

**You already know what you need to do. The difficult part is often starting.**

Your notes are somewhere on your computer. Your project is inside a folder. Your study material is a playlist. Your next task is in your head.

Senku brings that context together.

Create a **Space** for what you're working on, connect it to the folder where your work already lives, add the tasks and resources you need, and return to everything from one place.

> **“senku is a spark, you are the fire.”**

Senku doesn't try to manage your life or take control of your files.

**It simply reduces the friction between deciding to work and actually starting.**

---

## Core Features

### Spaces
Create separate environments for different areas of work. A Space can represent a semester, project, placement preparation, or anything else you want to keep separate. Each Space has its own folder, tasks, Rough Note, and workflow preferences.

### Tasks
Senku supports two ways of organizing work:
- **Scheduled** — work you want to do at a specific time, with reminders and optional automatic resource opening.
- **Flexible** — ongoing work that doesn't need a specific schedule.

Tasks can also be **Completed, Snoozed, Reassigned, or Pinned**, while unfinished and broken-resource tasks are tracked separately.

### Your Files & Resources
Senku works with the files you already have. Browse a Space's main folder, open files with their normal Windows applications, and link additional resources such as folders, files, playlists, and web pages to your tasks.

**Senku does not copy, move, sync, or reorganize your files.**

### Rough Note
Not every thought needs to become a task. **Rough Note** gives you a simple place for quick ideas, reminders, temporary thoughts, and daily brain dumps. Write it down and keep moving.

---

## Designed to Stay Out of Your Way

Senku is intentionally lightweight. There is no cloud account to create, no forced folder structure, and no new file-management system to learn.

Your files stay where they are. Your workflow stays yours.

Senku simply keeps the context of your work close: **what you're doing, where it lives, and when you want to return to it.**

---

## Local & Private

Senku is designed to work offline. Your files remain on your computer. Senku stores its own settings, tasks, paths, and notes locally.

There is:
- No cloud account
- No file synchronization
- No dependency on an online service
